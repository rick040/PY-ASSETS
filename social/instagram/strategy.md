# ParkingYou Instagram strategy

Version 1.0, 5 November 2026. The content library that goes with it is `content-library.json` (66 ready-to-make posts and reels), readable as `content-library.md` and as the Notion database "ParkingYou Instagram contentbibliotheek".

## 1. Where we are

Numbers from `dashboard.social_posts` (Instagram, 2026):

| Format | Posts | Reach per post | Average |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reel | 4 | 275 (Bos en Lommer), 209 (NN Marathon), 196 (Koningsdag), 95 (NN Marathon) | **194** |
| Static post | 5 | 107 (Pasen eieren), 97 (Carnaval), 56, 56, 38 (GLOW) | **71** |

What this tells us:

1. **Reels reach about 2.7 times more people than static posts.** That matches how Instagram ranks content in 2026. Reels are the default; static posts are for things people save (prices, overviews).
2. **We only post around campaigns.** Between May and September 2026 there was nothing on Instagram. An account that goes quiet for months loses reach on the next post too. This is the main reason for a backlog.
3. **The best performers are concrete and local**: one location, one price, one reason ("Straatparkeren in Amsterdam? Bos en Lommer vanaf €2 per 30 min"). Generic national posts do less.
4. **Each post is published on Instagram and Facebook at the same time.** Keep doing that; the library works for both.

## 2. Goal

Post three times a week, every week, so the account is never quiet, and turn reach into reservations at the 14 locations that take online bookings.

| Goal | Measure | Target after 12 weeks |
|---|---|---|
| Consistency | Posts per week | 3, no gap longer than 7 days |
| Reach | Average reach per reel | 400 (2x today) |
| Interest | Saves and shares per post | 5 or more on carousels |
| Action | Link clicks from the bio link, reservations with UTM `ig` | Baseline in week 4, then +20 percent |

## 5. Format mix and rhythm

| Format | Share | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Reel (Remotion animation, 6 to 15 s) | 60% | Highest reach. Built from PY-ASSETS, so every reel looks like our signs. |
| Carousel | 20% | Gets saved: overviews per city, how-tos, price lists. |
| Static post | 20% | Price posters, typographic posts, real photos. |

**Weekly rhythm (3 posts):**

| Day and time | Slot | Typical pillars |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday 19:00 | Reel | P2 price, P3 how-to |
| Thursday 12:00 | Carousel or post | P1 location, P6 subscription |
| Friday 17:00 | Reel | P1 location, P4 weekend or event |

Stories: 3 to 5 a week, mostly reposts of the feed plus polls (P7). Keep fixed highlights: "Locaties", "Zo werkt het", "Storing?", "Abonnement".

## 6. How to use the backlog

When there is nothing planned, pick from the library with these rules, in order:

1. **Event in the next 14 days?** Take the P4 item for it (or `IG-046` "Evenementen deze maand"). The event calendar is in the dashboard under Evenementen.
2. **Weather fits?** Rain: `IG-049`. Frost: `IG-053`. Summer holiday: `IG-034`, `IG-052`.
3. **Otherwise rotate pillars**: pick the pillar that has been posted least in the last two weeks.
4. **Never two price posts in a row**, and no more than one P7 per week.
5. **Before posting anything with "Prijs check" ticked**, check the price in the kennisbank (`kennisbank.tarief`). Prices change every February (see the price change playbook), so after 1 February every price item goes back to "Prijs check".
6. **Mark the item as Gepost with the date.** Evergreen items can come back after 3 to 6 months with a new hook. Seasonal items come back next year with new dates.
7. **Add new ideas as rows in the library**, with pillar, format and template filled in, so the backlog keeps growing.

## 7. Copy rules

* Dutch, informal "je", short sentences, warm and down to earth. A little local humour is fine ("Salaai!" with carnaval).
* The hook carries the post: it is the first frame of the reel and the first line of the caption (within about 125 characters).
* Every price and number comes from the kennisbank. Say "vanaf" when it is the lowest of several prices. Name the location next to the price.
* "Zeker van je plek" and "reserveer" only for the 14 locations where online booking is possible (Bos en Lommerplantsoen, Cruquius, Plantage, Zeeburgerkade, DLL garage, DLL parkeerdek, Evoluon, Philips Bedrijfsschool, Philips Stadion, TD Gebouw, Parking At the Park, Europoint, Hofplein, Shopping Center). For the others: "route" or "meer info" in bio.
* Emoji only as markers in a list of facts (📍 🅿️ 💶 🔌 🗓️), never as decoration in every line.
* No long dashes, no "Agree?", no "Say goodbye to", no made-up reviews or numbers of customers.
* End with one call to action: "Reserveer via de link in bio." Make sure the bio link (Linktree or a landing page) goes straight to the booking page of the location in the latest post.
* 3 to 5 hashtags: `#ParkingYou` + city + one topic (`#parkeren`, `#dagjeuit`, `#besparen`). Tag partners (`@gloweindhoven`) only with their approval.
* Third-party marks (EasyPark, Pathé, GLOW, payment brands) only from their official files and only where the partner agrees.

## 8. Visual rules (from PY-ASSETS)

* Colours: PY-Blauw `#1A3580` as the main background, PY-Aqua `#32B9CD` for keywords and accents, white text. PY-Oranje `#F08300` only as a rare accent (new location, carnaval). The blue to aqua verloop for backgrounds.
* Type: Ubuntu only. Key phrase Bold, supporting words Regular on the same line (observed rule 2 in `docs/print-rules.md`).
* Shapes: the speech bubble (`py-atom-shape-bubble-*`) as photo frame and price label, pills for destinations, the figure set (`py-atom-figure-*`) for people. These are the same shapes people see on the signs at the location; that recognition is the point.
* Logo on navy or a photo: white with the aqua dot. On light blue: all white with a white dot. Never the navy ring fill of the `-reverse` logo files.
* Reels: 1080 x 1920, important text outside the top 250 px and bottom 330 px, frame 0 is never empty (it is often the cover), everything readable without sound, end with url-chevron "parkingyou.nl" and the logo.
* Feed versions of reels: 1080 x 1350 (4:5).

R1, R5 and R7 together cover 25 reels. The scene plans, props and PY-ASSETS IDs per template are in `content-library.json` (`templates`) and the brand reference for building is `brand-parkingyou.md`.

Proposed setup: a Remotion project in `social/remotion/` in this repo, reading SVGs from `svg/`, fonts from `fonts/` and colours from `tokens.json`, with one composition per template in two sizes (Reel and Feed) and the library item ID as input, so `npm run render -- IG-017` renders that item.

Static posts (S1 to S5) can be built as HTML pages rendered to PNG with Playwright, or in Canva from the same assets.

## 10. Measuring and adjusting

* Weekly, in the dashboard (Marketing, Social): reach, saves, shares per post. Note the top 3 and bottom 3 with one sentence why.
* After 4 weeks: compare pillars. A pillar that is below half the average reach gets fewer slots for the next 4 weeks; the best pillar gets one extra.
* After 8 weeks: test 2 hooks for the same item (for example `IG-017` "Zelfde plek. Drie prijzen." against "€10 korting voor één klik eerder").
* Add the Instagram UTM (`utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<item id>`) to the bio link so reservations can be traced back to an item.
