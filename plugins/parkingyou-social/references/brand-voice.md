# Brand voice for social

Sources: `ST` = `social/instagram/strategy.md` §7, `CS` = `brand-2027/02-build/core-spec.md` §1 and §10, `PR` = `docs/print-rules.md`, `D` = `decisions.md`,
and the approved copy in `social/instagram/content-library.json`, the 15 posted statics and `applications.json`.

## 1. Who is speaking

ParkingYou talks like a helpful local who knows every garage: warm, down to earth, a little humour, never pushy.
The reader is someone on their way somewhere (shopping, the zoo, a match, work). Parking is the means, not the story: talk about the moment they are going to.

## 2. Rules

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Language | Dutch | ST 7 |
| Address | "je" and "jij", never "u" | D S7 |
| Sentences | Short. One idea per sentence. Headline at most about 8 words | ST 7, CS 10 |
| Hook | The first frame of a reel, the headline of a post and the first line of the caption say the same thing, within about 125 characters | ST 7 |
| Concrete | A number, a place or a time beats an adjective: "700 plekken. Onder het stadion." | ST 1, CS 10 |
| Prices | Always with the location next to it. "vanaf" when it is the lowest of several prices. Theme `feed`: "€12,50"; theme `2027`: "€ 12,50". Never ",-" | ST 7, CS 10 |
| Times | "18:00 tot 23:00", 24-hour clock | CS 10 |
| Reserve wording | "Reserveer", "zeker van je plek" and the "Reserveer online" button only for the 14 reservable locations (`product-truth.md`). Others: "route" or "meer info" | ST 7 |
| Call to action | One per post: "Reserveer via de link in bio." (reservable) or "Meer info via de link in bio." | ST 7 |
| Positive framing | Say what is easy, not what is wrong with the reader. "Jij stapt droog uit", not "Ben jij ook altijd nat?" | PM-138 |
| Local humour | Allowed in small doses and only where locals say it ("Salaai!" with carnaval in Eindhoven, "Lampegat") | ST 7 |
| Dashes | No em dashes, no spaced en dashes | D S9 |
| Emoji | Only as markers at the start of a fact line in the caption (📍 🅿️ 💶 🔌 🗓️ 🎟️). Never in a visual or headline | D S8 |
| Exclamation marks | At most one per caption; none in headlines except a local cheer | ST 7, CS 10 |
| Brand name | "ParkingYou" in running text; the logo spells "parkingyou" | PR §3 |
| Hashtags | 3 to 5: `#ParkingYou` + city + one topic (`#parkeren`, `#dagjeuit`, `#besparen`) | ST 7 |
| Tags | Partner accounts (`@gloweindhoven`) only with their approval | ST 7, D S10 |

## 3. Words

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| plek, parkeren, reserveren, inrijden, je auto staat droog | parkeerfaciliteit, parkeervoorziening, mobiliteitsoplossing | Spoken language |
| vlak bij, naast, onder, op loopafstand, X minuten lopen | centraal gelegen, uitstekende bereikbaarheid | Concrete beats vague |
| voordelig, goedkoper, je bespaart €X | spotgoedkoop, de goedkoopste, nummer 1 | No superlatives without a source |
| zonder gedoe, geregeld, zo naar binnen | zorgeloos genieten, naadloze ervaring, ontdek het zelf | AI-sounding filler |
| Reserveer via de link in bio | Klik hier, Mis het niet, Wees er snel bij | Pushy |
| (nothing) | Agree?, Say goodbye to, Zeg maar dag tegen, game changer, next level | Template phrases (ST 7) |

## 4. Good and bad lines

Good lines come from approved posts and print. Bad lines are rewrites that break a rule.

| Good | Bad | What is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Naar Blijdorp? **Parkeer ernaast.** | Op zoek naar dé perfecte parkeerplek bij de dierentuin?! | Vague, superlative, double punctuation |
| 2,70 meter. **Ook je busje past.** | Ruime garage met uitstekende doorrijhoogte. | Abstract; the number is the hook |
| Het regent. **Jij stapt droog uit.** | Ben jij ook altijd zeiknat als je gaat shoppen? | Negative framing about the reader |
| Vroeg boeken = **tot 1/3 goedkoper** | Bespaar nu megaveel met vroegboeken! | Hype, no number |
| Gaat de slagboom niet open? **Bel ons.** | Storing? Geen paniek! 😱 Wij helpen u graag verder. | Emoji, "u", drama |
| Met de trein naar Rotterdam? Parkeer voor **€3,64**. | P+R Schiedam: dé slimme keuze voor forenzen — goedkoop en makkelijk. | Em dash, no price, "dé" |
| Overdag weg? 's Avonds en in het weekend **je vaste plek**. | Avond/weekendabonnement vanaf €39,42 p/m (excl. servicekosten, voorwaarden van toepassing). | Reads like a price list; the hook is the situation |
| Je plek staat klaar. (2027 offer template) | Uw parkeerplaats wordt voor u gereserveerd. | "u", passive |
| Kabel terughangen **na gebruik** (print, PR 2) | KABEL TERUGHANGEN NA GEBRUIK!!! | All caps, shouting |

## 5. Caption shape

```
<hook, same as the headline, max ~125 characters>

<1 or 2 short sentences: the situation and why this location helps>

📍 <location, city>
💶 <price with unit>                 (only with price_check done)
<one more fact marker if useful>

<one CTA line: Reserveer via de link in bio. / Meer info via de link in bio.>

#ParkingYou #<Stad> #<onderwerp>
```

Alt text: one or two plain sentences that describe what is in the image and repeat the headline and price. No hashtags, no emoji, no "afbeelding van".
