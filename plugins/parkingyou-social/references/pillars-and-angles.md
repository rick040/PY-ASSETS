# Pillars and angles

Source: `social/instagram/strategy.md` §3 to §6 and `social/instagram/content-library.json` (66 items, IDs `IG-001` to `IG-066`).
Use this file to come up with a post when there is no brief, and to check that a plan stays balanced.

## 1. The seven pillars

| ID | Pillar | Share | Goal | Best formats | Main measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Locatie in beeld | 25 % | One location: name, city, 2 or 3 facts, its best price | Reel R1, post S1/S2, carousel per city | Reach, profile visits, link clicks |
| P2 | Slim voordelig | 20 % | Make the saving concrete: ter plaatse vs online vs vroegboeken, strippenkaart, doelgroeptarieven, P+R | Reel R2, post S1/S4, carousel | Saves, link clicks |
| P3 | Zo geregeld | 15 % | Remove friction: reserve, drive in, scan, pay, find your car. Show the real stickers and signs | Reel R4/R5/R7, carousel | Saves, watch time, fewer support questions |
| P4 | Erbij zijn | 15 % | Parking for the moments people go into town; reusable per event, every year | Reel R3/R6, post S1/S4, story sequence | Reach, shares, bookings around the date |
| P5 | Zorgeloos parkeren | 10 % | Covered, cameras, charging, your spot is ready | Reel R7, post S2, carousel | Reach, saves |
| P6 | Je vaste plek | 10 % | Subscriptions for commuters, residents and businesses; the sum that shows when it pays off | Reel R2, post S1/S5, carousel; LinkedIn (D S5) | DMs, subscription requests |
| P7 | Achter de slagboom | 5 % | ParkingHosts, sign design, polls, humour, actions | Reel R5/R7, post S4/S5 | Comments, shares, followers |

## 4. Picking rules (from `strategy.md` §6)

1. Event in the next 14 days: take its P4 item (or IG-046).
2. Weather fits: rain IG-049, frost IG-053, summer holiday IG-034 and IG-052.
3. Otherwise the pillar posted least in the last two weeks.
4. Never two price posts in a row; at most one P7 per week.
5. A price item needs a kennisbank check on the posting day.
6. Mark the item "Gepost" with the date in Notion. Evergreen items can return after 3 to 6 months with a new hook.
7. New ideas become new rows in the library with pillar, format and template filled in.
