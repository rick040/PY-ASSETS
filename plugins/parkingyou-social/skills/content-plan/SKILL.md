# ParkingYou content plan

1. **Read** `references/pillars-and-angles.md` (pillars, shares, picking rules, weekly rhythm) and `social/instagram/content-library.json`.
2. **What was posted.** Query the Notion database "ParkingYou Instagram contentbibliotheek" for status and posting dates. Items posted in the last 3 months are out unless they are seasonal and due again.
3. **Calendar.** List events and seasons in the period (the dashboard event calendar if available, otherwise the `timing` field of the library items). Events in the next 14 days come first (rule 1).
4. **Fill the slots:** Tuesday 19:00 reel, Thursday 12:00 carousel or post, Friday 17:00 reel, plus 3 to 5 stories. Apply the picking rules in order: event, weather, least-posted pillar, never two price posts in a row, at most one P7 a week.
5. **Check the mix** against the pillar shares (P1 25 %, P2 20 %, P3 15 %, P4 15 %, P5 10 %, P6 10 %, P7 5 %) over the period, and flag gaps.
6. **Output** a table: date and time, item id, pillar, format, archetype or reel template, hook, price check needed (yes/no), open check from `strategy.md` §11 if any. New ideas get a proposed new row for the library (pillar, format, template, hook, caption draft).
7. **Do not write to Notion** unless the user asks; then add rows with status "Gepland".
