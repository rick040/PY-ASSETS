# ParkingYou 2027: core spec

Version 2.0, 2026-11-07. Status: Gate 1 direction A with task colours, revised at Gate 2 to variant 1 "Rustig" with own identity
(Rick: "Ga voor variant 1, werk het verder uit. Ik mis wel wat eigen identiteit. Wees wat meer creatief"). Version 2 changes are marked **(v2)**.
This file is the single source of truth for the 2027 brand system. Every chapter, figure, template and token is built from it.
Tokens in machine form: `brand-2027/tokens/tokens.json` (W3C DTCG 2025.10), `tokens.css`, `contrast.md`, all generated by
`brand-2027/tools/build_tokens.py`. If this file and the token file ever disagree, this file wins and the generator is fixed.

**Change control.** Workstreams may not invent values (colours, sizes, spacing, radii, type sizes, durations, ratios) outside this file.
If a chapter needs a new value, it adds it to a "Voorgestelde waarden" section at the end of its chapter file with the reason,
and the creative director decides. Until approved, a proposed value is not used in finals.

## 1. Brand principles

The guideline states these in Dutch, in this wording.

1. **Een merk met een gezicht.** Het hoofd van onze parkeerders heeft de vorm van ons beeldmerk: het logo wordt een mens. We praten met je, niet over je.
2. **Kleur betekent iets.** Navy is het merk en rijden. Aqua is ons accent en lopen. Oranje zie je alleen waar je betaalt. We gebruiken kleur niet als versiering.
3. **Leesbaar eerst.** Licht tegen donker, groot genoeg, in elke garage en op elk scherm. Elke tekstkleur haalt WCAG 2.2 AA.
4. **Rustig en vlak.** Veel wit, grote letters, vlakke kleur. Per vlak het logo en hooguit twee van onze elementen. Geen verloop, geen schaduw op merkvormen.
5. **Echt.** Eigen garages en straten, echte prijzen, gewone taal. Geen stockgevoel, geen beloftes die we niet waarmaken.

## 3. Grid, unit and spacing

**Format unit u (print, signage, social, motion).** u = short side of the format divided by 20.
Examples: business card 85 x 55 mm, u = 2.75 mm. A4, u = 10.5 mm. A2, u = 21 mm. Lightbox 1000 x 1000, u = 50 mm. Post 1080 x 1350, u = 54 px. Reel 1080 x 1920, u = 54 px.
- Margin 2u on all sides (bleed is extra). Gutter 1u.
- Columns: 6 on portrait and square formats, 12 on landscape formats.
- Vertical rhythm: elements snap to multiples of 0.5u.
- Reels: keep the top 250 px and bottom 340 px of 1920 free of key content (platform UI), as in `remotion/`.

**Screen (UI).** 4 px base. Spacing tokens `space.0` to `space.10`: 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128 px.
Layout grid: phone 4 columns, 16 px gutter, 16 px margin; tablet (768) 8 columns, 24 px gutter, 32 px margin; desktop (1200+) 12 columns, 24 px gutter, max content width 1200 px.
Touch targets at least 44 x 44 px (WCAG 2.5.8 asks 24; we use 44).

**Radius.** `radius.none` 0 (signage panels, photos in layouts), `s` 4, `m` 8 (inputs), `l` 16 (cards, sheets), `xl` 24, `pill` 999 (buttons, chips, route boards).
Print: cards and passes follow ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 corner radius 3.18 mm. Signage panels: square corners, except route boards (pill, as today).

**Elevation (screen only).** `shadow.1`, `shadow.2`, `shadow.3`, all PY-Diep based (see tokens). Never on the bubble, the logo or print.

## 4. De bubbel

The bubble is the outer silhouette of the beeldmerk (path in `kit/assets/bubble.svg`, viewBox 36 x 43.423). Figure: `00-core/figures/bubble-anatomy.png`.

- **Never redrawn, never stretched.** Uniform scaling only. No rounded or flattened variants.
- **Sizes.** Width 3u, 5u, 8u or 13u in format units; on screen 24, 40, 64 or 104 px. Larger only as a cropped background graphic (rule below).
- **Fills and content colour (fixed pairs).**

| Fill | Content colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| navy `bubble.navy` #1A3580 | white | drive, levels, brand moments on light grounds |
| aqua `bubble.aqua` #32B9CD | PY-Blauw | walk, and the brand accent on navy grounds |
| orange `bubble.orange` #F08300 | PY-Diep #0D142E | **(v2)** only on payment signs and terminals in the garage |
| white `bubble.white` #FFFFFF | PY-Blauw | on navy, aqua and photo grounds; outline 1/30 of width in PY-Blauw on white grounds |
| photo | none | people and places, never text inside |

- **Content.** One item: a level ("-1", "0", "2"), a section ("1A"), a price, one pictogram, or one word of at most 6 characters ("Vrij", "Info"). **(v2)** No arrows in a bubble: arrows stand alone.
  Content sits centred in the circle part, inside a square of 60 percent of the width. Numbers use tabular figures, weight 800.
- **(v2) Always upright.** The bubble is the beeldmerk's silhouette, so like the logo it is never rotated or mirrored. Direction is shown by the angular arrow, never by the tail.
- **Count.** One bubble per surface. Two on surfaces of 1 m or more in either direction, or on multi-panel layouts (pylon, overview board), never touching.
- **Clear space.** 1u around the bubble (screen: 8 px for 24 and 40 px bubbles, 16 px for larger).
- **(v2) No background bubble.** Large decorative bubbles are retired; that role goes to "het vak" (section 4b).
- **Never:** gradient fill, shadow, 3D, pattern fill, text longer than 6 characters, the logo inside, two bubbles overlapping, a bubble as a photo frame with text over it, a bubble in safety or RVV signs.
- **Motion (v2).** The bubble pops in: scale 0.9 to 1 with `easing.bubble`, `duration.expressive` 600 ms, no rotation. Brand moments only (reel open and close, app splash, confirmation). Under reduced motion: fade 0 to 1 in 120 ms.

## 4b. Own elements (v2)

Five elements, each with one task. **Per surface: the logo plus at most two of them.** Reference figure: `00-core/figures/identity-elements.png`.

| Element | Task | Source |
|---|---|---|
| De parkeerders | the brand's face; people, welcome, moments | `svg/atom/py-atom-figure-*`; kit `figure-*-navy.svg`, `figure-*-white.svg` |
| De stem (halve boog) | opens a message; separates modules on signs | `svg/atom/py-atom-shape-bracket-arc` (arc only); kit `arc.svg` |
| Het vak | background: a parking deck from above, one bay is yours | drawn from the rules below |
| De bubbel | price, level, availability | section 4 |
| De hoekige pijl | direction | `svg/atom/py-atom-arrow-*-plain`; kit `arrow-*` |

**De parkeerders.** The nine handbook figures, drawing unchanged: loper (walk), zwaaier (wave), wijzer (point), danser (dance), springer (jump), shopper (bags-walk), juichende shopper (bags-cheer), gastheer wijst (cap-point), gastheer zwaait (cap-wave).
- Colour **(D41)**: always one colour. PY-Blauw on white and light grounds and on PY-Aqua (`figure-*-navy.svg`), white on PY-Blauw (`figure-*-white.svg`). The head keeps its beeldmerk bubble shape in the same colour; it is never coloured separately.
- Size: height 3u to 8u in layouts; on a pedestrian sign up to 60 percent of the face height; on screen 48 to 160 px.
- Always standing on a line: a text baseline, a rule of 0.1u, a format edge, or the floor line of a scene. Never floating, never rotated, never cut off.
- One figure per surface. May be mirrored to face the route or the text. Never inside a bubble, never with a speech text, never in safety, RVV or ISO 7010 signs, never as a pictogram for a route (pictograms stay the 48 grid set).
- Roles: loper on pedestrian routes and entrances; zwaaier for welcome and goodbye; wijzer and gastheer wijst next to an arrow or contact details; springer and juichende shopper for confirmations and deals; shopper for city and event campaigns; danser for events only.

**De stem (halve boog).** The arc from handbook 1.4 (bracket arc), without the slogan.
- Opens a message: placed left of a headline or a key line, its height equal to the text block it opens (one to three lines), gap 0.3x its height. Never closed by a second arc, never on the right.
- Stroke 1/25 of the arc height, at least 1.5 px or 0.5 mm. Colour PY-Aqua on white and PY-Blauw; PY-Blauw on PY-Aqua.
- On signs: the separator between modules (P and arrow, pictogram and text), same stroke as above, PY-Aqua on navy, PY-Blauw on aqua.
- One per surface in layouts; on signs as many separators as there are module breaks.

**Het vak.** A parking deck seen from above. Vertical bay lines at a pitch of 4u (screen: 72 px), line weight 0.05u (screen 2 px), running in from the top or bottom edge for 6u to 8u (screen 144 to 192 px), with an optional dashed lane line (dash 0.3u, gap 0.25u). **(D40)**
- Exactly one bay is "jouw vak": filled `vak.mine`, its two side lines in PY-Aqua at double weight, closed at its open end by a stop line (same weight, PY-Aqua). It holds one figure standing on that stop line, or one bubble, nothing else. **(D40)**
- Colours: on PY-Blauw lines `blue.400`, jouw vak `blue.600`; on white lines `neutral.200`, jouw vak `aqua.100`.
- Use: social, newsletter header, card backs, posters, screen headers and empty states. Never on wayfinding signs or behind text.

**De hoekige pijl.** The handbook arrow (`py-atom-arrow-right-plain`, `-downleft-plain`), rotated in steps of 45 degrees. Colour = the content colour of its ground. Stands alone, never in a circle or bubble. In buttons and links at the cap height of the label. Tabler icons are used for everything else, never for arrows.

### 5.2 Colour roles

**Task colours (wayfinding, terminals, UI meaning).**

| Task | Ground | Content | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rijden | PY-Blauw | white | portals, entrance and route lightboxes, car route boards, level markers, height bars keep red/white |
| Lopen | PY-Aqua | PY-Blauw | pedestrian route boards, lift, stairs, exit to street, pedestrian entrance (as bubble on navy face) |
| Betalen | PY-Oranje | PY-Diep | **(v2)** only in the garage: payment machines, tariff panels, fault and storing notices. Not on screens, not in marketing |

- Lightbox faces and portals stay navy. On a lit face the task colour is carried by the bubble, never by a full translucent aqua or orange face.
- Printed boards (Dibond, foil) may carry the task colour as the full ground.
- Safety and escape signs (ISO 7010, NEN 3011) and RVV traffic signs are never brand coloured. Brand colours never sit next to them as decoration.
- Status always uses a word next to the colour (Vrij, Vol, Gesloten). Colour alone never carries meaning.

**(v2) Marketing, screen and print.** Approximate share of a layout: white and paper (`blue.50`) 47, PY-Blauw 38, PY-Aqua 13, orange 0 percent (garage payment only, about 2 percent of a signage set).
Aqua appears as the stem, the head of a parkeerder, jouw vak lines, a bubble or a small accent. Outside the garage, aqua is the brand accent; inside the garage aqua always means walking.

**(v2) Verloop retired.** No gradients anywhere. Existing gradient signs are replaced by flat PY-Blauw at their next renewal.

### 5.3 Contrast rules

- Text needs 4.5:1, large text (24 px or 19 px bold and up, print 18 pt or 14 pt bold) and UI graphics 3:1. All semantic pairs are computed in `tokens/contrast.md`; every one passes.
- Allowed text pairs on brand grounds: white on PY-Blauw 11.26, PY-Blauw on PY-Aqua 4.80, PY-Diep on PY-Oranje 6.87, PY-Blauw on white 11.26, `aqua.700` on white 4.90, `orange.700` on white 4.80.
- Not allowed: white on aqua (2.34), white on orange (2.64), aqua text on white (2.34), orange text on white (2.64), PY-Blauw on orange below large size (4.26).
- Signage: only white on PY-Blauw, PY-Blauw on PY-Aqua and PY-Diep on PY-Oranje, all matte.

### 5.4 Semantic tokens (screen)

Names and values for light and dark are in `tokens.json` under `semantic.*` (dark values in `$extensions.nl.parkingyou.modes.dark`).
Groups: `surface` (default, subtle, raised, brand, inverse), `text` (default, muted, brand, on-brand, link, aqua), `border` (subtle, strong),
`focus` (ring, ring-on-brand), `action` (primary, secondary with hover and pressed), `task` (drive, walk, pay with fg), `bubble` (navy, aqua, orange, white),
`status` (free, full, closed), `feedback` (error, error-bg, success, warning, info). Components read only semantic tokens, never primitives.

## 6. Typography

- **Family: Ubuntu Sans**, variable, axes wght 100 to 800 and wdth 75 to 100, Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0, free for print, web and signage. Files in `brand-2027/fonts/`.
  Cap height 0.693 em, x-height 0.518 em, tabular figures available.
- **Logo** stays Ubuntu outlines; never retype the wordmark.
- **Weights in use:** Regular 400, Medium 500 (UI only), SemiBold 600 (labels), Bold 700, ExtraBold 800 (display, numbers in bubbles).
  Condensed (wdth 75) only for signage names that do not fit at normal width; never in running text.
- **Fallback:** "Ubuntu Sans", Ubuntu, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Arial, sans-serif. Office and email: Arial.
- **Rules:** sentence case; no italics; no all caps except eyebrows of at most 3 words (letter spacing 0.08 em); tabular figures for prices, times, levels.
  The voice pattern stays: the key phrase bold, the rest regular, in one line ("**Parkeren** zonder gedoe."); when the stem opens it, the line may break after the key phrase. **(D39)**

## 7. Iconography (frame for B3)

- **(v2) Arrows:** always the angular handbook arrow (section 4b), never a Tabler arrow or a chevron.
- Two sets, one drawing logic. **Pictograms** (signage, large UI): solid shapes on a 48 unit grid, 4 unit safe zone, existing 12 handbook pictograms redrawn to this grid.
  **UI icons** (screen): outline, 24 px grid, 2 px stroke, round caps and joins, 2 px safe zone, sizes 16, 20, 24, 32.
- Icon colour follows the text colour of its ground. On a bubble, the bubble content colour.
- Third-party marks (RVV, ISO 7010, payment brands, partners) are used as supplied, never redrawn in our style.

## 8. Motion (frame for B3)

Tokens: `duration.fast` 120, `moderate` 200, `slow` 320, `expressive` 600 ms; `easing.standard`, `enter`, `exit`, `bubble`.
UI motion is productive (fast, standard). **(v2)** Expressive motion: the bubble pop (no rotation), the stem drawing itself from top to bottom (`duration.slow`, `easing.enter`), and the parkeerders changing pose by a straight cut between two figures (for example loper to juichende shopper, as in `remotion/` story 2), never animated limbs, never rotated or tilted. Autoplay longer than 5 s needs a pause control. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
Reels: one idea per reel, 6 to 15 s, logo end card 1.5 s, captions always on (body cap height 0.4u, white on PY-Blauw or PY-Blauw on white).

## 9. Imagery stance (frame for B4)

Own garages, entrances, streets and people arriving or leaving, in daylight or clean garage light, natural colour, no colour overlays, no stock lifestyle, no AI images of real locations.
Photos sit full bleed, in a plain rectangle or in the bubble mask. Text never sits on a busy photo area; use a navy field instead of a darkening overlay.

## 10. Voice (for all copy)

Dutch, je/jij on every channel (the booking surface moves from u to je). Short sentences. Concrete numbers. Prices as "€ 12,50" with a space, decimal comma, no ",-".
Times "18:00 tot 23:00". No em dashes, no emoji, no exclamation marks in wayfinding. Buttons: verb first ("Reserveer je plek", "Betaal").
Bilingual signage: Dutch first, English below at the same size, regular weight, separated by 0.5u.

## 11. Production

- Print in CMYK (FOGRA39 coated or FOGRA52 uncoated) with 3 mm bleed; signage in the supplier's RIP with the RAL and vinyl references from B2.
- Matte laminate on every sign face. Gradients only on printed media.
- Variable font for design; suppliers receive outlined artwork or named static instances (Regular, Bold, ExtraBold, Condensed Bold).
- Before colour sign-off: one sample board set tested in a real garage under its own light (open item from R4).
